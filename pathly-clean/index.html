How It Works

From first click to personalized plan

Explore freely first. Only create an account when you're ready to save your results.

01

Pick your career

Choose from 20 career paths. See BLS salary data, 10-year job growth, and whether your state requires licensing — instantly.

02

Add your schools

Type any institution. Pathly detects CC + University combos and checks for formal transfer agreements automatically.

03

Set your budget

A smart slider filters out pathways that exceed your limit before you ever see them. FAFSA and Pell Grant options surfaced automatically.

04

Enter your academics

Your GPA unlocks scholarship tiers. Transfer credits show a live savings preview — every credit hour you've earned is money you won't borrow.

05

Get your dashboard

Side-by-side pathway comparison across 6 dimensions, plus a semester-by-semester credit plan for your top path.

06

Save & share

Create a free account to save your results, update your profile, and share your plan with family or advisors.

Features

Everything you need in one place

Transfer articulation engine

Cross-references formal 2+2 agreements between community colleges and universities. Flags formal, TAG, case-by-case, or no agreement.

Semester-by-semester credit plan

Check off courses as you complete them. Live cumulative debt tracker updates in real time. Milestone markers for transfer and graduation.

State licensing compliance

Validates every pathway against your state board for nursing, teaching, social work, and other regulated professions.

Scholarship match engine

Surfaces Pell Grants, Bright Futures, merit scholarships filtered by your GPA, state, and career. Shows exact eligibility requirements.

10-year ROI projections

Interactive charts comparing debt accumulation, annual cost, and 10-year earnings vs. total investment across all pathways side by side.
